Ascendant calculates positions once, then derives everything else from them.
The same few terms appear in every function name, type, and guide, so they are
worth learning first.

## Input

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Moment** | The instant at which positions are calculated. It can be a birth, a transit, or any other event. It is always UTC and is separate from settings. |
| **Located Moment** | A Moment paired with a latitude and longitude. It is the input to every chart calculation. |
| **AstroParams** | The two settings of a calculation: the **ayanamsa** and the **house system**. They live outside the Moment, and every result records them. |

The **ayanamsa** aligns tropical longitudes to the fixed zodiac, and Ascendant
ships 47 predefined ones. The **house system** decides the cusps of D1, and
Ascendant supports 13. The default is KrishnamurtiVP291 with Placidus. See
[Choose a school and settings](/docs/guides/settings).

## Positions and charts

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Placements** | The exact sidereal longitudes and motion states of the Lagna and the grahas at a Located Moment, with each star and pada. Every chart and every other calculation is derived from them. |
| **Chart calculation** | The Placements plus the requested charts, derived together under one set of AstroParams. It always contains D1. |
| **Division** | A chart's identity in the divisional system: D1, D2, D3, and so on up to D60. |
| **D1 chart** | The chart whose houses start at the house system's cusps. Only D1 carries the chart angles, the KP star, sub and sub-sub lords, significations, and ruling planets. |
| **Divisional chart** | A chart made by mapping the Lagna and grahas through a Division. Its houses are whole signs counted from the mapped Lagna, whatever the house system. It has no angles or KP data. |
| **Planet** | A graha as placed in one chart: its longitude, degree, sign, dignity, and retrograde state. Its star and pada stay on the Placements. |
| **Sign** | One of the twelve zodiac signs, with its lord. |
| **Degree** | A position within a sign, from 0 up to but not including 30. In a divisional chart it is the degree the mapping produces. |

Rahu and Ketu are grahas that behave like planets in every way except that
they do not rule a sign.

Relationships run one way. A house lists its planets, but a planet does not
carry its house. To find a planet's house, scan the chart's houses for it.

Because you may know these ideas by other names: a **kundali**, a
**horoscope**, or a **natal chart** is a Chart calculation. "Natal" is a meaning
you give a calculation made for a birth moment, not a kind of chart.

## Schools

Ascendant implements more than one tradition, and a single judgment never
mixes them.

| School | Typical settings | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **KP** | KrishnamurtiVP291 ayanamsa, Placidus houses | The default. It uses D1's cusps, star lords, sub lords, and significators. |
| **Parashari** | Lahiri ayanamsa, whole-sign houses | Used for promise, quality, and divisional charts. |

Always state the school and settings alongside a result.

## Timing and scoring

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Dasha** | A timeline of periods. Vimshottari gives planetary Mahadashas and Antardashas. Chara and Sthira give Jaimini sign periods. You can ask which period is active at any date. |
| **Ashtakavarga** | A Parashari scoring system built from the sign positions of the Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, and Lagna. |
| **Bhinnashtakavarga (BAV)** | The twelve sign scores for one of those eight entities, counted in **bindus** (benefic points). |
| **Sarvashtakavarga (SAV)** | The twelve sign scores formed by adding the seven planetary BAV tables. It totals 337 under the classical table. |
| **Shodhya Pinda** | A weighted total for one planet, from its reduced BAV after Trikona and Ekadhipatya reduction. |
| **Transit search** | A time-ordered search for the next or previous events of one graha. The events are sign ingresses, longitude hits, house-cusp crossings, and retrograde stations. |

## Jaimini

Jaimini is a school, not a single calculation. You request each result by
name.

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Chara Karakas** | Seven planetary roles assigned in descending order of degree within a sign. Planets at the same degree share the roles they tie for. |
| **Rashi Drishti** | The signs a reference sign influences under Jaimini's sign aspects. |
| **Karakamsha** | The D9 sign occupied by each planet holding the Atmakaraka role. |
| **Arudha Pada** | The sign reached by repeating the distance from a house's sign to its lord. |
| **Upapada** | The Arudha Pada of the twelfth house. |
| **Argala** | The supporting and obstructing relationships around a reference sign, or around Ketu in the reverse direction. |

## Provenance

Charts, the Jaimini results, the Chara and Sthira dashas, and transit events
carry their **provenance**: a `school`, a `method`, and a `version`. Each one
points to a **method specification** in the library's registry, which lists the
calculation steps and the criteria used to verify it. That is how a result can
be checked and repeated. Placements, Vimshottari dashas, and Ashtakavarga
results do not carry provenance. See [Provenance and verification](/docs/guides/provenance).
