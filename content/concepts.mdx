Ascendant calculates positions once and derives everything else from them.
These terms appear in every function name, type, and guide.

## Input

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Moment** | The instant positions are calculated for: a birth, a transit, or any other event. Always UTC, and separate from settings. |
| **Located Moment** | A Moment with a latitude and longitude. The input to every chart calculation. |
| **AstroParams** | The two settings of a calculation: the **ayanamsa** and the **house system**. They live outside the Moment, and every result records them. |

The **ayanamsa** aligns tropical longitudes to the fixed zodiac. Ascendant
ships 47. The **house system** sets the cusps of D1. Ascendant supports 13.
The default is KrishnamurtiVP291 with Placidus. See
[Choose a school and settings](/docs/guides/settings).

## Positions and charts

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Placements** | The exact sidereal longitudes and motion states of the Lagna and the grahas at a Located Moment, each with its star and pada. Every chart and calculation derives from them. |
| **Chart calculation** | The Placements plus the requested charts, derived together under one set of AstroParams. Always contains D1. |
| **Division** | A chart's identity in the divisional system: D1, D2, D3, and so on up to D60. |
| **D1 chart** | The chart whose houses start at the house system's cusps. Only D1 carries the chart angles, the KP star, sub and sub-sub lords, significations, and ruling planets. |
| **Divisional chart** | A chart made by mapping the Lagna and grahas through a Division. Its houses are whole signs counted from the mapped Lagna, whatever the house system. No angles or KP data. |
| **Planet** | A graha as placed in one chart: longitude, degree, sign, dignity, and retrograde state. Its star and pada stay on the Placements. |
| **Sign** | One of the twelve zodiac signs, with its lord. |
| **Degree** | A position within a sign, from 0 up to but not including 30. In a divisional chart, the degree the mapping produces. |

Rahu and Ketu are grahas that behave like planets except that they rule no
sign.

Relationships run one way. A house lists its planets, but a planet does not
carry its house. Scan the chart's houses to find a planet's house.

A **kundali**, **horoscope**, or **natal chart** is a Chart calculation.
"Natal" is a meaning you give a calculation made for a birth moment, not a kind
of chart.

## Schools

Ascendant implements more than one tradition. A single judgment never mixes
them.

| School | Typical settings | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **KP** | KrishnamurtiVP291 ayanamsa, Placidus houses | The default. Uses D1's cusps, star lords, sub lords, and significators. |
| **Parashari** | Lahiri ayanamsa, whole-sign houses | For promise, quality, and divisional charts. |

Always state the school and settings with a result.

## Timing and scoring

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Dasha** | A timeline of periods. Vimshottari gives planetary Mahadashas and Antardashas, split further into Pratyantardashas and Sookshmas. Chara and Sthira give Jaimini sign periods. Query the period active at any date. |
| **Ashtakavarga** | A Parashari scoring system built from the sign positions of the Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, and Lagna. |
| **Bhinnashtakavarga (BAV)** | The twelve sign scores for one of those eight entities, counted in **bindus** (benefic points). |
| **Sarvashtakavarga (SAV)** | The twelve sign scores from adding the seven planetary BAV tables. Totals 337 under the classical table. |
| **Shodhya Pinda** | A weighted total for one planet, from its BAV after Trikona and Ekadhipatya reduction. |
| **Transit search** | A time-ordered search for the next or previous events of one graha: sign ingresses, longitude hits, house-cusp crossings, and retrograde stations. |

## Jaimini

Jaimini is a school, not a single calculation. Request each result by name.

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Chara Karakas** | Seven planetary roles assigned in descending order of degree within a sign. Planets at the same degree share the roles they tie for. |
| **Rashi Drishti** | The signs a reference sign influences under Jaimini's sign aspects. |
| **Karakamsha** | The D9 sign occupied by each planet holding the Atmakaraka role. |
| **Arudha Pada** | The sign reached by repeating the distance from a house's sign to its lord. |
| **Upapada** | The Arudha Pada of the twelfth house. |
| **Argala** | The supporting and obstructing relationships around a reference sign, or around Ketu in the reverse direction. |

## Provenance

Every result carries its **provenance**: a `school`, a `method`, and a
`version`. Each points to a **method specification** in the library's
registry, which lists the calculation steps and the criteria that verify it.
That is what makes a result checkable and repeatable. See
[Provenance and verification](/docs/guides/provenance).
