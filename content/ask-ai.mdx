A horoscope is a set of tables. An AI assistant can help you read them, answer
questions about your career, relationships, and timing, and explain the terms. The
[chart page](/chart) gives you two ways to hand your horoscope over: a button that
opens an assistant with your chart already in the message, and a PDF you can keep or
attach yourself.

You need to [create a horoscope](/docs/horoscope) first. The buttons appear under
your results.

## Ask an assistant with one click

Under your results, **Ask ChatGPT** is the main button. Its menu also offers **Ask
Claude** and **Ask Gemini**. Choosing one opens a new browser tab with your chart
already written into the message.

| Button | Opens |
| --- | --- |
| **Ask ChatGPT** | ChatGPT at `chatgpt.com` |
| **Ask Claude** | Claude at `claude.ai` |
| **Ask Gemini** | Google AI Studio at `aistudio.google.com` |

<Callout type="info" title="Gemini opens AI Studio">
  The Gemini app cannot be given a message through a link, but Google AI Studio can.
  So **Ask Gemini** opens AI Studio, not the Gemini app. You may need to sign in with
  a Google account.
</Callout>

You may also need to sign in to ChatGPT or Claude. Each assistant decides whether it
waits for you to press send or sends the message as soon as the page opens, so read
the message before you leave the tab.

## What the message contains

The message is built from your horoscope. It starts with instructions and then your
chart.

| Included | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Instructions** | A request to read the Ascendant astrology skill from GitHub, with a link to its reference files. The skill tells the assistant how to give a reading. |
| **Your name** | Only if you entered one. |
| **D1** | Your Rashi chart: each planet's sign, degree, and house. |
| **D9** | Your Navamsha chart. |
| **KP planets** | Each body's house, sign lord, star lord, sub lord, and signifying houses. |
| **KP cusps** | Each house's sign, cusp, and its lords. |
| **Vimshottari** | Your Mahadasha periods with their start and end dates. |
| **Current Antardasha** | The sub-periods of the Mahadasha you are in today. |

It does **not** include your birth date, birth time, or birth place. Those are not
written into the message.

<Callout type="warn" title="Your chart is in the link">
  The whole message travels in the address of the tab that opens, so it is sent to
  the assistant's website and can be saved in your browser history. It contains your
  name, if you gave one, and the full chart. Planet positions can also point to an
  approximate birth moment. Use a name you are comfortable sharing, and do not use the
  buttons on a shared computer if that is a concern.
</Callout>

## Make the most of it

The assistant starts from your chart, so you can ask in plain words. A few places to
begin:

- "What does my current Vimshottari period emphasize?"
- "Explain my Rashi chart to me as if I knew nothing about astrology."
- "What do the sub lords in my KP chart say about my career?"
- "When does my next Mahadasha begin, and what changes?"

How the readings work, from the Ascendant skill:

- **One school per answer.** KP is the default. Ask for **Parashari** by name if you
  want promise, quality, yogas, or divisional charts. The assistant states which it
  used, and the two are never mixed.
- **Evidence is cited.** The reply gives its judgment first, then shows the method
  underneath.
- **No claims about death, serious accidents, or severe illness.** The skill
  declines to time or assert them.
- **It says when evidence is missing.** Expect "Insufficient evidence" rather than a
  guess.

An assistant's reading is guidance to reflect on. It is not certainty, and it is not
medical, legal, or financial advice.

## Save as PDF

**Save as PDF**, beside the Ask button, downloads your horoscope as a file called
`birth-chart.pdf`. Your browser builds it on your device when you click, so the PDF
is not generated on a server.

The PDF holds:

- Your **name, birth date and time, place, UTC time, and coordinates**, with the
  ayanamsa and house system used, and your Lagna.
- Your **placements** and the **divisional charts** that were calculated.
- The **Vimshottari, Chara, and Sthira dasha** tables.
- The **Jaimini** results: Chara Karakas, Karakamsha, Upapada, and Arudha Pada.
- The **KP chart** table.

Keep it, print it, or attach it to a conversation with any assistant that accepts
files. Unlike the Ask buttons, the PDF includes your birth details, so share it with
care.

## If something does not work

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Nothing opens when you click Ask | Allow pop-ups for the site, then click again. |
| The assistant opens with an empty box or a cut-off message | The message is long and travels in a link, and a service may shorten it. Save the PDF and attach it to a new conversation instead. |
| The assistant cannot open the skill link | Some assistants cannot browse. Ask it to give a general reading from the tables, and say which school you want. |
| You are asked to sign in | ChatGPT, Claude, and AI Studio need an account. Sign in, then use the button again. |

## Keep your chart for next time

The buttons start a fresh conversation each time. If you would rather save your chart
once and ask questions whenever you like, from your own coding assistant, install the
Ascendant skill. See [Skills and Plugins](/docs/skills).
