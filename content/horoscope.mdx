The [chart page](/chart) turns a birth date, time, and place into a full Vedic
horoscope in a few seconds. It is free, and you do not need an account. This page
walks you through the form, explains what you get back, and tells you what happens
to the details you enter.

## What you need

Have these four things ready:

- **A name.** Any label you like. It appears on your chart and your PDF.
- **Your birth date.**
- **Your birth time.** The more exact, the better. See [Why the time matters](#why-the-time-matters).
- **Your birth place.** The town or city where you were born.

## Create your horoscope

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the form

Go to the [chart page](/chart) and choose **Enter birth details**. A form opens.

</Step>
<Step>

### Fill in the details

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Required. A label for the chart. |
| **Birth date** | Type a date such as `23 Jul 2003`, or open the calendar and pick one. |
| **Birth time** | The time on the birth record, as hours and minutes. |
| **Gender** | Optional. Choose Male, Female, or Prefer not to say. |
| **Birth place** | Start typing, then **pick your place from the list that appears**. |

<Callout type="warn" title="Pick the place from the list">
  Typing a place is not enough. The form needs you to choose one of the
  suggestions, which tells it the exact location. If you skip this, it says
  **Enter a birth place.** Add the state or country to your search if your town
  shares its name with others.
</Callout>

</Step>
<Step>

### Create it

Choose **Create Horoscope**. The button shows **Creating Horoscope** while it works
and **Chart ready** when it is done. Your horoscope appears on the page.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Why the time matters

Everything in a horoscope is measured from the moment and place of birth, and the
rising sign, called the **Lagna**, changes about every two hours. A few minutes can
move the Lagna's degree, and small changes show up most in the KP chart, where houses
are divided finely. Use the time on a birth certificate or hospital record if you
have one.

## Check the time zone

You enter the time as it was on the clock where you were born. The page works out the
time zone from the place you picked, for that date, and shows it beside your birth
time at the top of the results. Glance at it. If the zone looks wrong, you probably
picked the wrong place from the list, so choose **Edit details** and pick again.

If you see **That date and time do not exist**, the clock skipped that time on that
day, as it can when daylight saving begins. Check the date and time on your record.

## Read your horoscope

Your results are in five tabs.

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Chart** | Your Rashi chart (D1), the main birth chart, and your Navamsha chart (D9). |
| **KP** | The same birth in the Krishnamurti Paddhati system: a wheel, a table of planets, the house cusps, and a dasha timeline. |
| **Dasha** | Your timing periods. Vimshottari is the common one, and the Chara and Sthira sign periods are alongside it. |
| **Jaimini** | The Chara Karakas, Karakamsha, Upapada, Arudha Pada, and the Rashi Drishti and Argala on your Lagna. |
| **SAV** | The Sarvashtakavarga, a score for each sign. |

A few notes on reading them:

- The **Chart** tab uses the Lahiri ayanamsa and whole-sign houses. The **KP** tab
  uses the Krishnamurti ayanamsa and Placidus houses. They are two different methods,
  so do not expect the houses in one tab to match the other.
- The **Dasha** tab lets you move between Vimshottari, Chara, and Sthira. Find the
  period you are in now to see your current phase.
- The **SAV** tab scores each sign by how much support it gives your planets. Higher
  numbers mean more support, and the page highlights the strongest signs and marks
  the sign where each planet is weakest.

## Change or reuse your details

Choose **Edit details** at the top of the results to correct anything and create the
horoscope again.

After you create a horoscope, your browser remembers those details, up to 20 people
in all. The next time, start typing the name in the **Name** field and pick it from
the suggestions, and the form fills in the date, time, and place for you. They are
kept on this device only.

## What happens to your details

- The **place search** runs in your browser and asks OpenStreetMap for matching
  places, so the place name you type is sent there.
- The **chart is calculated** by this site's calculation service, which receives the
  date, time, and location you chose.
- The **PDF is built in your browser** when you save it.
- The **saved details** stay in your browser's own storage. Clearing your browser
  data removes them.
- **Nothing is sent to an AI assistant** unless you choose to. See [Take your
  horoscope to an AI assistant](/docs/ask-ai).

## What a horoscope can and cannot tell you

A horoscope shows positions and periods calculated from your birth moment. It is
guidance to reflect on. It is not certainty, and it is not medical, legal, or
financial advice.
